Part 0 · Decide this first

Our art style

Every picture, sprite, menu and effect in the game will follow one art style, so we lock it before anything else gets drawn. The direction so far: cute, chunky anime that feels modern, juicy and shiny, in our rainbow prism palette (the same family as Maze so Puzzle and SSS), with gel, slime, liquid glass and holographic shine.

Decided on 31 Oct: a blend of A and C (C's bold, chunky shapes with A's smooth, polished shine), no texture, medium inked lines, about 4 heads tall. It's written up in docs/ART_STYLE.md.

Blend test result (1 Nov): Blend 3 won, with clear outlines kept in every picture. The first card shows three ways to mix A and C, each drawn as three real game moments: a story picture, the exploring screen and the dance battle. Vote for the mix that feels most like our game. If one mix is best for story pictures and another for playing, choose "Mix them" and say which.

See them big: Blend 1 story · explore · dance  |  Blend 2 story · explore · dance  |  Blend 3 story · explore · dance

For Amelia

We picked "bold and chunky, plus shiny". Now there are three ways to mix them. Look at all three rows. Which row looks the most like our game?

Part 1 · Story

Bring back balance

The angels serve a kind goddess. The demons have pushed too far toward heaven, and the world has tipped out of balance. The heroes don't destroy the demons. They push them back to where they belong, so heaven and the world are even again.

  • Happy ending: balance is restored.
  • No scary horror and no realistic violence.
  • Amelia already said no to the sad ending idea.
For Amelia

The demons got too greedy. Our angels help everyone go back to their own place, and then everyone is happy.

Part 2 · Heroes

Two little Tenshi

One pretty anime girl angel and one cute young boy angel. Each player controls one. They walk, jump, and help each other. Neither one is "player 2". They are a team.

For Amelia

You and a friend each get an angel. You need each other to win.

Part 4 · Mode A · Explore

A sunny Roman diorama

One short side-scrolling level, more like a playable toy box than a big stage. Both angels are on screen at once.

  • Move left and right, and jump
  • A few easy obstacles
  • Ends at the fountain where you meet the Mermaid

Done when: both players can reach the fountain, and it clearly looks like Rome.

For Amelia

Hop across the columns to find the Mermaid at the fountain. What should Rome have in it?

Part 5 · Creature

Our Mermaid friend

She lives in the Roman fountain and does sparkly water magic. Her tail is glitter jelly and her fins are like holographic glass. She must look cute as a friend and still be able to help in a battle, with an outline that's easy to see.

For Amelia

This is your Mermaid. You get to choose what she's like, her colour, and her name.

Part 6 · Mode B · Rhythm

Dance to power up

A very short song, about 20 to 30 seconds. Each player has one button. Press it on the beat. It's the easiest mode only, and you can't really fail.

  • Both players help
  • Big, happy feedback on every hit
  • At the end the Mermaid powers up, and you can see it

Done when: each player helps, and the Mermaid looks different afterwards.

For Amelia

Press your button when the music says so. The Mermaid dances and gets super powers.

Part 7 · Mode C · Battle

One tiny magical skirmish

The two angels are the generals. The upgraded Mermaid fights beside them against one small group of demons. When you win, the fort switches back to your side.

  • Both angels can move
  • The Mermaid helps by herself
  • Clear "you won!" moment, and the fort changes

Done when: you can win, and the fort clearly shows it's reclaimed.

For Amelia

The demons took a fort. You, your angel friend and the Mermaid win it back.

Part 8 · How we build it

Humans choose, AI builds

The AI helpers build one small piece at a time, then stop and show you. Nothing counts as approved just because an AI made it. These are the stops:

  5. Rhythm game
  6. Battle
